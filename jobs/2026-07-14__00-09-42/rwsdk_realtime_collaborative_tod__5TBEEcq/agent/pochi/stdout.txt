### How realtime works
When any client adds a to-do, `setTodos` sends the new array to the `SyncedStateServer` Durable Object over a WebSocket (managed by the `useSyncedState` hook). The Durable Object broadcasts the update to all other subscribers, causing their `todos` state to update and React to re-render — no polling, no manual reload.
