5. **Concurrency control**: an `asyncio.Semaphore(3)` bounds in-flight jobs to 3; `asyncio.gather(...)` fans out the per-file tasks.
6. **Polling**: `await client.extract.get(job_id)` every 2 seconds (non-busy-wait) until `status == "COMPLETED"`, raising on `FAILED`/`CANCELLED`/timeout.
7. **Outputs**:
