## What it does

1. **Feature extraction** (`librosa`): for every WAV, loads at a fixed `sr=22050`, computes four feature families, then concatenates the per-frame **mean** and **std** (over time) of each:
   - MFCCs (`n_mfcc=20`) → 20 mean + 20 std
   - Spectral contrast (`n_bands=6` → 7 bands) → 7 mean + 7 std
   - Zero crossing rate → 1 mean + 1 std
   - Spectral centroid → 1 mean + 1 std

   This yields a fixed 58-D vector per clip, identical order for train and test.

2. **Training**: stacks the 8 training vectors (4 speech, 4 music), standardizes them with `StandardScaler` (important — MFCC magnitudes are ~100s while ZCR is ~0.1, so scaling prevents the large-magnitude features from dominating), then fits `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000)`.

3. **Prediction**: extracts the same features for each file in `/home/user/test/`, applies the same scaler transform, and predicts.

4. **Output**: writes `/home/user/predictions.csv` with header `filename,label` and one row per test WAV (basename only, no duplicates).
