```tsx
<Link to="/" activeProps={{ className: 'active' }} className="navigation-link">Home</Link>
<Link to="/about" activeProps={{ className: 'active' }} className="navigation-link">About</Link>
```

**Router wiring** — `src/main.tsx` uses `createRouter({ routeTree })` + `RouterProvider`, plus the `Register` interface augmentation for full type inference.

**Vite config** — `vite.config.ts` registers `TanStackRouterVite({ target: 'react' })` and sets `server.port: 4273`.
