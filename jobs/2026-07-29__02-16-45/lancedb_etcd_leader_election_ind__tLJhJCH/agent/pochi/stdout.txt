**Leader election (etcd v3 JSON gateway via `requests`)**
- `acquire()`: grants a bounded-TTL lease, then attempts an atomic `CREATE`-compare transaction (`create_revision == 0`) on the single election key bound to that lease. Only one candidate can win. On loss (or any error), the lease is revoked immediately so it never leaks.
- The fencing token is the etcd **global revision** at which the winning `Put` committed — since the key was just created, this equals its `create_revision`, and etcd revisions are a single monotonic counter across the whole keyspace, so tokens strictly increase across every term from every worker.
- A daemon thread refreshes the lease via `/v3/lease/keepalive` at `ttl/3` intervals while leader.

**Leadership queries (`is_leader`, `fencing_token`)**
- Both perform a live re-check against etcd (`/v3/kv/range` on the election key, comparing value/`create_revision` against our own worker_id/token) rather than trusting a local flag — this is what correctly turns leadership off once a TTL actually expires, even though the local keepalive thread was already stopped (e.g. after `simulate_crash`).

**Fenced maintenance (`run_maintenance`)**
- Raises `PermissionError` unless the live etcd check confirms current leadership; re-checks again immediately before calling `table.optimize()` to narrow the TOCTOU window. Returns `worker_id`, `fencing_token`, `version_before/after`, and `unindexed_before/after` (via `table.version` and `table.index_stats(...)`). Verified idempotent — repeated calls preserve row count and eventually leave the table fully indexed.

**Failover**
- `resign()`: deletes the election key and revokes the lease — a waiting follower's `acquire()` succeeds immediately.
- `simulate_crash()`: stops the keepalive thread only, leaving the lease to expire naturally after `ttl` seconds; followers cannot win until then, matching real crash semantics.
- `close()`: stops local threads/session only, does not touch etcd state.
