| Fully offline HTML | `chart.save(..., inline=True)` — zero CDN references confirmed |

The `frame=[-29, 0]` window spans the current row plus the 29 preceding observations (30 total), grouped and sorted by `symbol` + `date` so each symbol's rolling mean is computed independently in the correct chronological order.
