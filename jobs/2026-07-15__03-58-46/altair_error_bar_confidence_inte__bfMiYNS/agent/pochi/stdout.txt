| Layer | Mark | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw observations** | `mark_circle` (semi-transparent) | `transform_calculate` generates `(random()-0.5)*0.4` and feeds it into the `xOffset` channel — this jitters each point ±0.2 band-widths horizontally without changing the axis labels |
