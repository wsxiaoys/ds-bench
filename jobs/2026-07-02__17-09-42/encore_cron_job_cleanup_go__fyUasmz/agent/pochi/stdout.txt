  - `//encore:api public method=POST path=/records` → inserts a record (parses `created_at` from RFC3339 string into `time.Time`).

- `cleanup/cleanup.go` exposes:
  - `//encore:api public method=POST path=/cleanup` → `DELETE FROM records WHERE created_at < $1` with cutoff `time.Now().Add(-24*time.Hour)`, returning `{"deleted_count": N}`.
