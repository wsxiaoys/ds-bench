## Pipeline (`/home/user/classifier.py`)

**Feature extraction** (per file, with `librosa.load(sr=22050)`):
- `librosa.feature.mfcc(n_mfcc=20)` → 20×mean + 20×std = 40 dims
- `librosa.feature.spectral_contrast(n_bands=6)` → 7×mean + 7×std = 14 dims
- `librosa.feature.zero_crossing_rate` → 1×mean + 1×std = 2 dims
- `librosa.feature.spectral_centroid` → 1×mean + 1×std = 2 dims

Concatenated in that order → 58-dim feature vector (identical for train and test).

**Model**: `sklearn.preprocessing.StandardScaler` followed by `sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000)`. Feature scaling was essential because the raw features live on very different scales (MFCCs ~hundreds, ZCR ~0.05, centroid ~1000–3000); without it the centroid feature dominated and the model missed the speech-vs-music boundary.

**Inference**: Test files are listed in sorted order, duplicates are removed, predictions are written to `/home/user/predictions.csv` with the required `filename,label` header.
