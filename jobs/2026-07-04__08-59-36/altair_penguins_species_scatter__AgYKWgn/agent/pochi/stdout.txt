## What the script does

**1. Registers the `penguins` dataset in `vega_datasets`**

The installed `vega_datasets` 0.9.0 release does not ship with the penguins dataset (it was added in newer npm releases of `vega-datasets`). The script registers a small `_Penguins` subclass of `vega_datasets.core.Dataset`, points its `base_url` at the jsDelivr CDN that hosts `penguins.json`, and patches `Dataset._dataset_info` and `DataLoader._datasets` so that `vega_datasets.data.penguins.url` resolves to a working URL.

**2. Builds the chart** with the required encodings:

| Channel | Field | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `x` | `Flipper Length (mm)` | `:Q` (quantitative) | `scale(zero=False)` — not anchored at 0 |
| `y` | `Body Mass (g)` | `:Q` (quantitative) | `scale(zero=False)` — not anchored at 0 |
| `color` | `Species` | `:N` (nominal) | 3 distinct species colors |
| `shape` | `Sex` | `:N` (nominal) | distinct shapes per sex; nulls safely ignored |
| `tooltip` | 4 fields | — | `Species`, `Island`, `Flipper Length (mm)`, `Body Mass (g)` |

- `mark_point(filled=True, size=80)` — filled circles at size 80
- `.interactive()` — adds an interval selection bound to scales, enabling pan & zoom

**3. Saves** the resulting chart as a self-contained HTML file at `/home/user/myproject/chart.html` via `chart.save(...)`.
