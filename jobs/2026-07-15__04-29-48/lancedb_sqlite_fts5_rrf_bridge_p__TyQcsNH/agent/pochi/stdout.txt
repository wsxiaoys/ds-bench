## What it does

The module-level `search(query, query_vector, k, keyword_weight, title_weight=1.0, body_weight=1.0)` function:

1. **Reads config** from `/app/data/config.json` (cached per process) to discover `sqlite_path`, `lancedb_uri`, `table_name`, `distance_metric`, and `rrf_k` — nothing is hard-coded.
2. **Pulls ≥50 keyword candidates** from the SQLite FTS5 table via `WHERE <table> MATCH ? ORDER BY bm25(<table>, <title_weight>, <body_weight>), rowid LIMIT 50`. The explicit `rowid` tie-breaker and 1-based ordering by returned position ensure determinism.
3. **Pulls ≥50 vector candidates** from LanceDB via `.search(query_vector).distance_type(distance_metric).limit(50)`, then re-sorts by ascending `_distance` with an `id` ascending tie-break for determinism.
4. **Fuses** the two ranked lists with the exact weighted RRF formula:
   `fused_score(d) = keyword_weight * 1/(rrf_k + bm25_rank) + (1-keyword_weight) * 1/(rrf_k + vector_rank)`, where absent documents contribute 0 for that store's term.
5. **Ranks** by fused score descending, breaking ties by document id ascending, and returns the top `k` as `list[dict]` with keys `id` (int) and `score` (float).
