It must support soft delete, tombstone-aware nearest-neighbour search, restore, and a garbage-collection pass that hard-deletes aged tombstones and physically reclaims space. ## Implementation Hints * Use the `lancedb` Python client with a local database directory. The rows must remain physically present.
