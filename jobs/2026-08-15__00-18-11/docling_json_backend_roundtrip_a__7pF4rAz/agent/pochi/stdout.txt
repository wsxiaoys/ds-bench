### Implementation Details:
1. **Candidate Discovery**: The CLI discovers candidates directly inside `--input-dir` that end with `.json`, ignoring sub-directories and non-JSON files.
2. **Classification & Recovery**: Candidates are audited and classified into exactly one status (`malformed_json`, `not_an_object`, `version_mismatch`, `schema_invalid`, `unreadable`, `ok`) in the required order.
   - For `version_mismatch`, a single recovery retry is performed by replacing the top-level `version` field with the installed model's schema version.
   - Successfully recovered candidates are marked as `recovered: true` and keep their status as `version_mismatch`.
3. **Artifact Generation**: For every successfully ingested candidate, normalized JSON and Markdown artifacts are written to `<out-dir>/normalized/<stem>.json` and `<out-dir>/markdown/<stem>.md`.
4. **Validation & Stability**:
   - `stream_parity` is evaluated by comparing path-based loading against stream-based loading.
   - `roundtrip_stable` is evaluated by re-ingesting the emitted normalized JSON and comparing its exported dictionary against the original.
5. **JSON Report**: A deterministic, byte-identical JSON audit report is produced at `<out-dir>/audit_report.json`.
6. **Exit Codes & Output**:
   - Standard output prints the summary string `AUDIT total=<total> ok=<ok> recovered=<recovered> failed=<failed>` as its final non-empty line.
   - Exit codes follow the rules precisely: `5` (invalid input-dir/no candidates), `4` (parity/roundtrip failure), `3` (failed candidates > 0), `0` (success).
