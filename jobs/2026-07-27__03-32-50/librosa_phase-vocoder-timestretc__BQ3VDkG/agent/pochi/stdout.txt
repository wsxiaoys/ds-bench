    seg=y[lo:hi]

**Architecture (hybrid transient-preserving phase vocoder):**
1. **Transient detection**: spectral-flux onset-strength envelope (STFT hop=512) + `librosa.onset.onset_detect` with `backtrack=True`, giving one detection per genuine broadband onset (verified: no spurious floods on tone-only or multi-click test signals).

3. **Stationary blocks** are processed with a true STFT phase vocoder (`librosa.stft` → `librosa.phase_vocoder` → `librosa.istft`, correct phase propagation) to perform the time-stretch (rate = original/target length) or pitch-shift (classic stretch-then-resample technique, phase-vocoder based).
4. **Transient blocks are copied verbatim** (byte-exact) from the input — this guarantees zero smearing of sharpness. Their placement in the output timeline is computed via anchor alignment (using the true peak-amplitude sample inside the window) so the transient lands exactly at `onset_sample × stretch_factor` (stretch case) or at the original `onset_sample` (pitch-shift case, since duration must stay constant).
5. Per-segment target lengths are chosen so cumulative output length exactly hits the required total (1.5× for stretch, exact original length for shift) — no post-hoc padding/trimming needed.
6. Short (3ms) non-overlapping edge tapers avoid boundary clicks without affecting timing.
