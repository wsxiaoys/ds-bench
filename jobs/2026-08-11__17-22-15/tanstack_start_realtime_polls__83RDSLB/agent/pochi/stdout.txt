     - Commits the transaction, or rolls back on any error.

   - `POST /api/polls` (`src/routes/api/polls.ts`): Creates a new poll. Validates the question and requires at least 2 non-empty options.

     - Option percentages with `data-testid="percent-<optionId>"`.
