Field Safety Handbook

This handbook describes the mandatory safety routine for every field technician operating remote relay stations. It is reviewed twice per year by the operations board and supersedes all earlier printed editions.

Incident Response

Any incident that interrupts service for more than five minutes must be reported to the duty coordinator before any repair attempt begins. The coordinator opens a ticket, assigns a severity, and confirms that the affected segment has been isolated.

Technicians must never re-energise a segment while a colleague is still working inside the enclosure. Confirm verbally, then confirm again with the lockout register.

Escalation Matrix

Severity one incidents escalate to the regional supervisor within fifteen minutes. Severity two incidents escalate at the end of the shift. All other findings are collected in the weekly maintenance digest.
