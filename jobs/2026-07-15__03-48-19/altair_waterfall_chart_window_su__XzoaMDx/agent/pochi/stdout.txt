## How it works

**Data loading (local only):** The CSV at `data/cash_flow.csv` (`label`, `amount`) is read with pandas and passed in-memory to `alt.Chart`. An explicit `order` column (`0..N-1`) guarantees the original file order is preserved everywhere — no remote URLs or `vega_datasets` are referenced.

**Window transform:** A `transform_window` computes the running cumulative total:
```python
cumulative = "sum(amount)",  frame=[None, 0],  sort=[alt.SortField("order")]
```
This gives each row's *new* cumulative balance (Begin→4000, Product A→5200, …, Tax→4400, End→4400).

**Calculate transforms** derive each floating bar's geometry:
- `y` (start) = `cumulative - amount` for normal rows; forced to `0` for the `End` row.
- `y2` (end) = `cumulative`.
- `bar_type` = `increase` / `decrease` / `baseline` (Begin & End).
- `text_val` = the delta amount, except `End` shows the grand total (cumulative).

The `End` row (amount `0`) therefore renders as the overall total bar spanning from `0` up to the grand total `4400`.

**Color coding** (three distinct colors via a fixed-domain scale):
- Green `#2E7D32` — increases (Product A, Services)
- Red `#C62828` — decreases (Product B, Refunds, Tax)
- Blue `#1565C0` — baseline/total (Begin, End)

**Floating bars** use both `y` and `y2` encodings so each bar starts at the previous cumulative total and ends at the new one.

**Text labels** are two filtered `mark_text` layers (so the pixel `dy` offset can differ per case): increase/baseline labels sit just above the bar top (`dy=-8`), decrease labels sit just below the bar bottom (`dy=16`), keeping them clear of the bar fills.

**Layered chart:** `bars + text_up + text_down` is a single layered Altair chart, exported via `Chart.save()` to a standalone HTML page that renders in any browser. The full Vega-Lite spec — including the inline dataset (`"datasets"`, no `"url"`) and the `window`/`calculate` transforms — is embedded in the file.
