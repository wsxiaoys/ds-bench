3. **Search & Conditional Ranking Logic (`rank.py`)**:
   - **Promotion Tier Sorting**: Modeled as a conditional score inside the `sort_by` parameter using Typesense's `_eval()` syntax:
     `_eval([ (badge:=sponsored):3, (badge:=featured):2, (badge:=none):1 ]):desc`
     This assigns discrete weights to the respective promotion badges, ensuring `sponsored` > `featured` > `none` as the dominant signal.
   - **Cross-Field Text Relevance**: To reward documents matching across multiple fields (both `title` and `description` rather than just one), we configure `text_match_type` to `"sum_score"`. This sums the field-level text match scores to form a comprehensive document-level relevance score. This score is then passed as the second tie-breaker in `sort_by` via `_text_match:desc`.
   - **Popularity Tiebreaker**: The final tie-breaker is popularity, passed as `popularity:desc`.
   - **Results Output**: The script accepts a search term via the `--query` CLI parameter, executes the request, and outputs **only** a JSON array of ranked document IDs to `stdout`.
