12. **`raw_error_list/2`** — Returns the untranslated, raw errors of the form at the given path as a list of `{field, message, vars}` tuples sorted by field name and message.
13. **`serialize/1`** — Generates a deterministic, plain-data snapshot of the entire form tree recursively, including name, id, type, resource, validity, values, hidden inputs, errors, and nested forms.
14. **`save/2`** — Submits the form and persists the whole order graph, reloading the persisted `Order` with its customer, line items, and modifiers preloaded in ascending `position` order. On failure, it returns `{:error, form}` carrying the validation errors.
