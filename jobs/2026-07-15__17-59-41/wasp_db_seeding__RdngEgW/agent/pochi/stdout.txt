## Idempotency
- **Users**: Looked up by their `username` auth identity (`findFirst` filtering on `auth.identities.some({ providerName, providerUserId })`). Existing users are reused; only missing ones are created. Since `User` has no unique natural key, this lookup-by-identity approach avoids duplicates on repeated runs.
- **Projects**: Used `prisma.project.upsert` keyed on the schema's `@@unique([userId, name])` compound key (`userId_name`), so the two projects per user are never duplicated.
- **Tasks**: Used `prisma.task.upsert` keyed on the schema's `@@unique([projectId, description])` compound key (`projectId_description`). On update it also re-applies the required `isDone` value, so the seed enforces the exact expected state every run.
