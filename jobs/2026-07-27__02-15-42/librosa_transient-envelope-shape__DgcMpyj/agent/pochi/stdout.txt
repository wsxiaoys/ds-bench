2. **Region & Gain Segmentation**:
   - The attack region spans `[s, s + attack_len)` where `attack_len` is converted to samples from `attack_ms`.
   - All other regions are classified as sustain regions.
   - Decibel gains are converted to exact linear amplitude multipliers (`10**(dB/20)`).

3. **Click-Free Crossfades**:
   - Boundary locations are detected automatically where the state switches between attack and sustain.
   - To make transitions click-free, a smooth cosine-based crossfade is applied, centered on each boundary and spanning `crossfade_ms` on each side.
   - To handle overlapping transitions (i.e. when transients are close together or near the ends of the file), the crossfade radii are dynamically bounded by the half-distance to the neighboring boundary, ensuring mathematically perfect continuity without any overlapping conflicts or instantaneous gain jumps.
   - Outside of these crossfade regions, the gain envelope is a flat plateau matching the exact target gain.

4. **Output Generation**:
   - The input waveform is multiplied by the continuous gain envelope.
   - Output samples are clipped to `[-1.0, 1.0]` to prevent digital clipping when writing to a file.
   - The shaped audio is written to the output path using `soundfile.write` maintaining the original sample rate and sample count.

5. **JSON Report**:
   - The script outputs a JSON report with the required keys: `sample_rate`, `num_transients`, `attack_gain_db`, `sustain_gain_db`, and an ascending `onsets` array detailing each detected onset time.
