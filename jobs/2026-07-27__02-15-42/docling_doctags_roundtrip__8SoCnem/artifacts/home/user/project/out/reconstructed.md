## Quarterly Financial Overview

This report summarizes regional revenue performance across the first two quarters of the fiscal year and documents the methodology used to compile it.

## Regional Revenue

The table below lists quarterly revenue by region, in thousands of dollars.

| Region   |   Q1 Revenue |   Q2 Revenue |
|----------|--------------|--------------|
| North    |         1200 |         1500 |
| South    |          900 |         1100 |
| East     |          700 |          850 |
| West     |         1300 |         1600 |

## Methodology

We aggregated figures from regional ledgers and reconciled them against bank statements.

## Data Sources

Ledgers were exported monthly from the accounting system and validated by the finance team.
