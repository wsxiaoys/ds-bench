3. **Structure & Heading Context Enrichment**:

5. **Output Generation**: The chunks are emitted as a UTF-8 JSON Lines file (`output/chunks.jsonl`) where each line contains exactly the four required keys: `id`, `heading_path`, `text`, and `page_no`.
